## 1. What Product Ops owns (the function)
Product Ops is the operational backbone that lets product teams scale. Common pillars:
- **Process & operating cadence** — roadmap/planning rituals, launch process, intake, prioritization frameworks, the "operating system" of the product org.
- **Tooling & systems** — own the product stack (Jira, Productboard, analytics), reduce tool fragmentation, run internal tools for PM/ops teams.
- **Data & insights** — instrument products, build dashboards, turn usage + feedback into decisions; close the customer-feedback loop.
- **Launch & enablement** — coordinate cross-functional GTM/launch readiness, write docs, drive internal + customer adoption.
- **Cross-functional glue** — align Product, Eng, Design, Data, GTM, Legal; remove blockers; keep one source of truth.

## 2. Frameworks & methods
- **Agile / Scrum / Kanban** — delivery cadence; sprint and backlog rituals.
- **Prioritization:** MoSCoW, RICE, weighted scoring, Kano; trade-off frameworks for roadmap calls.
- **Discovery & specs:** BRD / MRD / PRD authoring; user stories; acceptance criteria; UAT + exit criteria.
- **Operating models:** OKRs for goal-setting; RACI for ownership; stage-gate / launch-readiness checklists.
- **Lifecycle (PLM):** idea → spec → build → UAT → launch → hyper-care → iterate.
- **Continuous improvement:** PDCA / DMAIC for process redesign; service management (ITIL) where ops-heavy.

## 3. Tooling & ecosystem
- **Roadmap / PM:** Jira, Linear, Productboard, Aha!, Asana, Confluence.
- **Analytics / instrumentation:** Amplitude, Mixpanel, Tableau, Power BI, Looker; SQL for self-serve.
- **Feedback / research:** Productboard, Dovetail, Pendo, Gainsight (adoption), Zendesk (support signal).
- **Docs / enablement:** Confluence, Notion, Guru.
- **Takeaway:** Product Ops JDs prize tool fluency + the ability to *consolidate* tools and *standardize* process — name the stack you've actually run (Jira, Power BI, Tableau, SQL).

## 4. What's changing now (2026)
- **AI in the product-ops stack** — AI-assisted roadmapping, auto-summarized feedback, AI copilots in internal tools; Product Ops increasingly drives **AI tool adoption** internally (bridges nicely to `ai-operations.md`).
- **Do-more-with-less** — post-2024 efficiency focus elevates Product Ops as the team that raises PM throughput and cuts process waste.
- **Internal-tools PM** — a growing sub-niche: own the tooling product moderators/ops/support teams use (the Pinterest #088 shape).
- **Feedback-loop rigor** — closing the loop from usage data + customer signal to roadmap is the differentiator.

## 5. Core metrics — the product-ops menu
*(reach for the JD's metric; phrase your real result in that vocabulary)*

**1. Delivery & predictability · 交付与可预测性**
- *Def:* on-time delivery rate, cycle time / lead time, sprint velocity, release frequency, scope-change rate. 准时交付率/周期时间/迭代速度/发布频率。
- *Use:* prove reliable execution across concurrent workstreams. 证明多线并行的可靠交付。

**2. Process efficiency · 流程效率**
- *Def:* manual effort removed (hours/week), tool fragmentation reduced, onboarding/ramp time, automation coverage of a workflow. 人工时长削减/工具整合/上手时长/流程自动化覆盖。
- *Use:* the core Product Ops value — make the org faster and leaner. 让组织更快更省。

**3. Quality · 质量**
- *Def:* defect/escape rate, UAT pass rate, reporting accuracy, SLA compliance. 缺陷率/UAT 通过率/报告准确率/SLA 达成。
- *Use:* gate launches and protect data/reporting trust. 卡发布、守护数据可信度。

**4. Adoption & enablement · 采用与赋能**
- *Def:* internal/customer adoption rate, feature usage, training completion, time-to-proficiency. 内部/客户采用率/功能使用/培训完成/熟练时长。
- *Use:* a ship isn't a launch — show adoption you drove. 上线不等于落地，展示你拉动的采用。

**5. Roadmap & prioritization health · 路线图健康**
- *Def:* backlog age/size, % roadmap delivered, prioritization coverage (MoSCoW/RICE), resource/capacity utilization. 待办规模/路线图完成率/优先级覆盖/资源利用率。
- *Use:* show disciplined focus on high-impact work. 聚焦高价值工作。

**6. Business impact · 业务影响**
- *Def:* cost savings, utilization lift, productivity gain, budget variance, exec-reporting time saved. 成本节约/利用率提升/生产力/预算偏差/报告时长节约。
- *Use:* tie ops work to dollars and leadership decisions. 把运营工作连到钱与决策。

**Slicing dimensions:** team · product area · workstream · region · launch · quarter. 分层：团队/产品线/工作流/地区/发布/季度。

## 6. Standard dashboards & artifacts
- **Delivery / launch dashboard** — on-time rate, cycle time, release calendar, launch-readiness status.
- **Roadmap & backlog view** — prioritized backlog (MoSCoW/RICE), % delivered, backlog age.
- **Adoption dashboard** — feature usage + internal/customer adoption funnel.
- **Exec KPI dashboard** — the leadership roll-up (delivery, quality, cost, utilization).
- **Operating-cadence artifacts** — BRD/MRD/PRD, RACI, launch checklist, UAT plan + exit criteria, single source of truth.
