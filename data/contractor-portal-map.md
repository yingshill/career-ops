## How it works (so the map makes sense)
Big-tech/enterprise contractors are **W2 employees of a staffing vendor**, placed via an **MSP** (mostly **Magnit**). The candidate-facing layer is one of two platforms — **TalentNet** (`{company}.talentnet.community`) or **willhire** (`{company}.willhire.co`). If a company has one of these, you self-apply (this workflow). **If it doesn't, contractors go through staffing agencies/SOW** — that's the vendor channel, not this one (see Cisco below).
