## Send-day workflow (~90 min)

1. **Warm channels first.** 60-sec LinkedIn DM / Gmail search for original Insight Global / Majorel / Akkodis reps. Warm conversion ≈ 5× cold.
2. **Per named recruiter:** open LinkedIn profile → Message → paste universal message → swap `[name]` → send.
3. **Per agency intake portal:** submit resume in parallel (goes to central recruiter pool; back-contact in 24–48 hrs at biggest agencies).
4. **Agencies w/o named recruiter** (Vaco, Mondo): run LinkedIn search query → DM 2-3 connectable people.
5. **Log every send** in the Reply Log below.
6. **Follow up at +3-5 business days** to non-responders: *"Floating this back up — no rush, just want to stay on your radar."*
